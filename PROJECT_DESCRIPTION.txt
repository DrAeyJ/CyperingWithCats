Cyphering with Cats is an unserious project between two friends made for making up algorithms for cyphering data.
The point is literally just to make cyphering algorithms for the opponent and deciphering algorithms against opponent's cyphering algorithms.
Don't mind the spelling mistake in the name. I'm not entirely sure what's correct about this word.
The game consists of ten steps of algorithms with slowly rising difficulty.
As of May 30th, 2026, it is my turn to decipher my opponent's 3rd algorithm. He previously deciphered 3rd of mine.

Also, we both made websites powered by Flask and published using Cloudpub to access the cyphers and more.
